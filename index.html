a tiny mood diary

A little diary for how you're actually feeling.

Tap Mongle, pick how you feel, and that's it. Want to say more? Add a note or a photo. No ads, no pressure.

Free to download · iPhone and Android

Say hi to Mongle.

Mongle is a little cloud whose face changes with your mood. On rough days, it just keeps you company.

  • GreatGreat day!
  • GoodA nice, easy day.
  • OkayOrdinary days count too.
  • BadRough day, and that's okay.
  • AwfulYou got through today, and that's enough.

Takes about a minute.

  1. 01

    Check in fast

    Pick a mood, then tap how you felt and what you did. Writing is totally optional.

  2. 02

    Watch your month bloom

    Every day adds a petal in that day's mood color. By the end of the month, your feelings have grown into a flower.

  3. 03

    Keep every month as a gradient

    When a month ends, all its mood colors blend into one soft gradient. Collect twelve and that's your year.

  4. 04

    Find out what makes your day better

    Your stats show what tends to happen on your good days, like seeing friends or getting a workout in.
