Tap Mongle, pick how you feel, and that's it. Want to say more? Add a note or a photo. No ads, no pressure.

Free to download · iPhone and Android

Say hi to Mongle.

Mongle is a little cloud that changes with your mood. On rough days, it won't lecture you. It'll just hang out with you.

  • GreatGreat day!
  • GoodA nice, easy day.
  • OkayOrdinary days count too.
  • BadRough day, and that's okay.
  • AwfulThis will pass. Mongle's right here.

Takes about a minute.

  1. 01

    Check in fast

    Pick a mood, then tap how you felt and what you did. Writing is totally optional.

  2. 02

    See your month at a glance

    Each day turns into a little cloud on your calendar, so patterns pop out without you even trying.

  3. 03

    Find out what makes your day better

    Your stats show what's usually going on when you feel good. Seeing friends? Working out? Coffee?
