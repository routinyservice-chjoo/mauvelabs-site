Release notes

What's changed in each update. iPhone and Android updates sometimes come out on different days.

1.1.1 Android: Oct 23, 2026 · iPhone: Oct 24, 2026

Photos in backups

  • Photos in backups Backups now include your photos. Tap Save a backup in Settings › Your data to get everything in one file, free for everyone. Older CSV backups can still be restored.
  • Fixed feelings and activities you made before signing in showing a code instead of their name.
  • The What's new screen no longer pops up for people who just installed the app.
  • Improved stability when you unlock the app with App lock.
  • Small layout fixes.

1.1.0 Android: Oct 21, 2026 · iPhone: Oct 23, 2026

Widgets, Mongle Insights, and rest days

  • Widgets See today's mood and your streak right on your Home Screen, and tap to check in. On iPhone, you can add one to your Lock Screen too.
  • Mongle Insights Mongle spots patterns in your entries and sums them up for you. You get one insight a month for free, and Premium shows them all. All the stats you already had are still free.
  • Rest days Earn one for every 7 days you check in. The days don't have to be in a row. If you miss a day, one is used automatically so your streak keeps going. You can hold up to 2, or up to 4 with Premium. Premium also gives you an extra one every Monday.
  • Backup and restore Save your entries to a CSV file and restore them anytime, for free. You'll find it in Profile › Settings › Your data. Photos aren't included in the file.
  • Your own feelings and activities Add up to 5 of your own for free (feelings and activities combined), or as many as you like with Premium.
  • Longer notes Notes can now be up to 5,000 characters.
  • On this day Look back at what you logged on this date in past years.
  • Share my month Turn your monthly flower into a picture you can share. With Premium, you can also save it as a PDF. Your notes and photos aren't on it.
  • New themes Night garden, Lilac dusk, Sage, Morning sky, and Cherry blossom come with Premium. Classic is free.
  • 7-day free trial New subscribers can try Premium Yearly free for 7 days. If you have notifications on, we'll send a reminder the day before your trial ends.
