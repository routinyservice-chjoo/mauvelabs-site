1.1.3 Android: Oct 28, 2026 · iPhone: Oct 28, 2026

Gentler reminders

  • Logged today? We'll skip that day's reminder.
  • The check-in after 2 days without an entry now has its own switch in Settings. On days it's sent, your regular reminder is skipped, so it's one a day at most.
  • New reminders start at 8 PM. Your current times stay the same.
  • Haven't logged yet? Mongle may ask once if you'd like an evening reminder.
  • No reminders set up yet? We've turned on one quiet evening reminder. It only shows in Notification Center: no sound, no banner, no pop-up. Turn it off in Settings or right from the notification.

Photos in backups

1.1.0 Android: Oct 21, 2026 · iPhone: Oct 23, 2026

Widgets, Mongle Insights, and rest days

  • Widgets See today's mood and your streak right on your Home Screen, and tap to check in. On iPhone, you can add one to your Lock Screen too.
  • Mongle Insights Mongle spots patterns in your entries and sums them up for you. You get one insight a month for free, and Premium shows them all. All the stats you already had are still free.
  • Rest days Earn a rest day for every 7 days you log during a streak. Rest days pause the count instead of resetting it. If you miss a day, a rest day is used automatically so your streak keeps going. Rest days don't add to your streak, but they keep it alive. If you miss a day without one, your streak starts over at 0. You can hold up to 2, or up to 4 with Premium. Premium also gives you an extra one every Monday.
  • Backup and restore Save your entries to a CSV file and restore them anytime, for free. You'll find it in Profile › Settings › Your data. Photos aren't included in the file.
  • Your own feelings and activities Add up to 5 of your own for free (feelings and activities combined), or as many as you like with Premium.
  • Longer notes Notes can now be up to 5,000 characters.
  • On this day Look back at what you logged on this date in past years.
  • Share my month Turn your monthly flower into a picture you can share. With Premium, you can also save it as a PDF. Your notes and photos aren't on it.
  • New themes Night garden, Lilac dusk, Sage, Morning sky, and Cherry blossom come with Premium. Classic is free.
  • 7-day free trial New subscribers can try Premium Yearly free for 7 days. If you have notifications on, we'll send a reminder the day before your trial ends. Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial ends and you won't be charged.
